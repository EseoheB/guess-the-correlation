What is r?

The correlation coefficient r measures how closely two variables follow a straight line. Near 1, both rise together. Near -1, one rises while the other falls. Near 0, there is no straight-line pattern at all.

It is easy to misread. Lots of points can hide a weak pattern, and a few points can make a weak pattern look strong. Checking the number, instead of trusting your eye, is a habit worth building.
